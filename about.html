Origins

The Intersection of Craft and Intelligence

Headquartered between San Francisco and Berlin, LUMEN brings together acoustic engineers from top German transducer laboratories and industrial designers from the Bauhaus and Nordic traditions.

We discard planned obsolescence. Every battery compartment is designed for modular replacement. Every unibody aluminum chassis is milled from 75% recycled aerospace alloy. We build products you pass on, not throw away.
